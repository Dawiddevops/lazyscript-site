Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

LazyScript Lab respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how LazyScript and the LazyScript Publisher tool handle data when connecting to social media APIs such as TikTok.

2. Data We Collect

  • OAuth Tokens & Basic Profile Info: When an account owner authorizes LazyScript Publisher (e.g., via TikTok Login Kit with user.info.basic), we receive an OAuth access token and basic public profile identifiers strictly to verify the target channel and publish videos requested by the owner.
  • No Selling or Tracking: We do not sell, rent, or share user data or OAuth tokens with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. How Data Is Stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the authorized creator's private machine and are never exposed publicly.

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can revoke LazyScript Publisher's access at any time from your TikTok / Meta / Google account security settings, which immediately invalidates stored tokens.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact LazyScript Lab via youtube.com/@lazyscriptlab.
